# Shamanic Mounts 0.2.0 - In Your Tongue

The herd book, the items, and the mount screen follow your Minecraft language setting. Saves load as they are.

- **Ten languages.** Now available in Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Russian, Polish, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified). Spanish (Mexico) uses the same lines as Spanish (Spain). English stays the source, and a line with no translation yet stays in English.
- **Follows your Minecraft language setting.** Change language in Minecraft's Language menu. The mod has no language menu of its own.
- **Nothing else moved.** Taming, breeding, feeding, and the herd are unchanged.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21. Client and server.
