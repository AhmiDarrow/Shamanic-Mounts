# Shamanic Mounts 0.1.14 - Every Item Explains Itself

A polish pass. Nothing new to tame; everything you pick up now says what it is for.

- **Hints on the tack.** The Shamanic Saddle, Saddle Bags, Diamond Apple and Mount Trading Post each carry a grey line: how to calm and saddle a wild mount, what the bags hold and what happens when they come off, how to breed, how to offer a swap or a gift at the post.
- **Spawn eggs too.** Every founder egg says it hatches a wild mount of its line, that pelt and size lean with the biome's climate, and to calm it before the saddle.
- **Tooltips wrap** to the tooltip's width instead of running off the screen.
- **Mods list.** Shamanic Mounts links its CurseForge page and its issue tracker from the mods screen.
- **Pickaxe.** The Mount Trading Post is now tagged for the pickaxe, so it breaks at the speed a stone block should.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21. Client and server.
