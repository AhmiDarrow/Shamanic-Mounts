# Shamanic Mounts 0.1.0 - The Spirit Herd

Ten spirit mounts, each in its own biomes, bred gene by gene into a chimera.

- **Ten lines.** Eightfold, Drum hart, Elk, Crane, Nagual, Barghest, Roc, Shade, Bear, and Serpent, each with its own body, gait, and ridden gift: water-walking, a healing drum, a ram, gliding, a send-away, mob sight, flight, hiding and blinking, a maul, and a coil.
- **Brace to tame.** Right-click a wild adult with a Shamanic Saddle and hold jump through four jolts. Food does not tame a spirit mount.
- **Born where they live.** Each line spawns in its own biomes through tags built on the common `c:` biome tags, so modded biomes join in. The biome picks the pelt: polar bears on the snow, black bears in the forest, snow leopards on the slopes. The cold grows them a little larger.
- **Genes for everything.** Two copies of every gene. The body shows by rank and the hidden body blends its shape into the cross; the head, feet, and tail pick a copy at birth; size runs XS to XL and averages; pelts go A over B over C; wingspan averages from small to vast.
- **Breeding.** A Diamond Apple, eight diamonds around an apple, for each parent. The foal is born wild, keeps near the grown mounts, and takes the saddle once grown. Breed two mounts that carry every gift for an even chance at the chimera, a furry scaled dragon with all of them.
- **Tack and orders.** The classic mount screen with the saddle, Saddle Bags, and vanilla horse armor, and Follow, Stay, or Wander. Staying, a mount lies down. The elk's bags hold a third row.
- **Riding.** On a mount that hides, blinks, or dives, a held sneak keeps you in the saddle and a tap steps you off.
- **The herd book.** Every tame with its portrait, family links, and every gene with both parents' copies in breeder's notation; a gallery of the lines with their three pelts; a key that explains every rule; and a breeding chapter behind a spoiler switch.
- **Its own creative tab** with the saddle, bags, book, apple, and a spawn egg for every line.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21. Client and server.
