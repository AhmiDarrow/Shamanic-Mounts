# Shamanic Mounts 0.1.6 - Close at Heel

Your mounts come with you now: off the saddle, and through every portal.

- **Mounts follow by default.** A newly tamed mount follows you, and stepping off never changes its order. Barghests and bears no longer sit down by themselves when you dismount; a guard mount you told to stay still gets up to fight back. Choosing an order shows a confirmation above the hotbar.
- **Stay means stay.** A mount you tell to stay lies down again where you step off after a ride.
- **They cross with you.** When you change dimension (a portal, a teleport, the End exit), up to eight of your following mounts within 24 blocks come along and land beside you on safe ground, never in water, lava or leaves. A mount that is staying, leashed, carrying someone else or sent away stays behind. If a teleport throws you out of the saddle, you arrive back in it. A mount that has just crossed waits 15 seconds before it will take a portal, so it never walks straight back through the one you came out of.
- **Older saves.** Mounts left on the old default (Wander), and guard mounts left on the automatic stay, now follow. Any mount you told to stay keeps staying, and you can set any mount's order again on its saddle screen.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21. Client and server.
