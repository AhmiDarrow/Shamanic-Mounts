# Shamanic Mounts 0.1.11 - Small Foals

Foals are born small and grow into their size.

- **Foals start small.** A newborn foal is under a third of its grown size, hitbox and shadow included, and grows a step every minute until it is full size at twenty minutes. Each line keeps its proportions, so a roc foal is still a small roc and a bear cub still outweighs a steed foal. Before, a foal was full size from birth with only a larger head.
- **Foals already in your world** take the size for their age the next time they load.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21. Client and server.
