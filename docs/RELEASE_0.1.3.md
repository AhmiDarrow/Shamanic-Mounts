# Shamanic Mounts 0.1.3 - Condor Wings

Rocs and Cranes get real bird wings.

- **Spread wings like a condor's.** Broad from body to tip, with a deep, straight trailing edge of secondaries, clean rows of coverts with dark scalloped tips, and a wide flare of slotted primaries whose fingers stand apart and curl upward as the bird soars. The Roc's astral colour now shows only on its primary tips.
- **Folded wings that sit like a real bird's.** Standing, the wing drapes flat over the flank the way a resting vulture or eagle carries it: scalloped coverts at the shoulder, a pale covert band, dark flight feathers stepping down and back, and the primaries closing to a point past the tail. From behind, both wings hug the body.
- Flatter feathers throughout: no more chunky, stacked covert patches.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21. Client and server.
