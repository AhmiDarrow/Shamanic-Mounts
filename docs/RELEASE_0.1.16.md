# Shamanic Mounts 0.1.16 - A Meal for the Herd

Hurt mounts can be fed back to health. Saves load as they are.

- **Meat mends a tame.** Right-click a hurt tame with any meat in hand, raw or cooked, vanilla or from another mod. It eats one and heals by what the cut is worth: cooked beef more than raw, never less than a heart, never past full. Rotten flesh is refused.
- **Riding is unchanged.** A mount at full health ignores the meat and the click still mounts up. Wild mounts never take it, so food cannot tame anything; the Diamond Apple still owns breeding.
- **The book says so.** The Keeping section of the Herd Book explains the meal.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21. Client and server.
