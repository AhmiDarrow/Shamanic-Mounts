# Shamanic Mounts 0.1.7 - Light Hooves

The same herd, for far less.

- **Cheaper to look at.** Each mount draws in well under half the time it did, sending half the shapes, with an identical look. Shining eyes are drawn together once per frame instead of breaking up every mount's draw.
- **Smooth take-offs.** Winged mounts no longer rebuild their shape over and over as their wings open and fold or their tail fans out, which caused small hitches around a flock of wild fliers.
- **Lighter gifts.** The omen's glow is renewed only when it is about to fade, and hiding from mobs sweeps for watchers four times less often while turning away any mob that tries to target you in between.
- **Quicker to find you.** A mount remembers its owner instead of searching the player list several times a tick.
- **Smaller savings.** Flight stamina is no longer sent to every nearby player each tick, and new mounts no longer build their starting genes twice.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21. Client and server.
