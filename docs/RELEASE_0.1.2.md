# Shamanic Mounts 0.1.2 - Into the March

Wild mounts now roam Tribal Power's March, each line in the lands that suit it.

- **Mounts in every March biome.** With Tribal Power installed, wild mounts spawn across the March: Eightfold and Drum Harts on the Steppe, Elk, Bears, Rocs and Barghests in the Highlands, Rocs, Nagual and Shades on Glimmer Ridge, Elk, Bears and white Harts in the Snow Fields, Cranes, Serpents and Nagual in the Reed Fen, Cranes and Serpents in the Shallows, Shades, red Harts and Eightfold in the Crystal Fields, and red Rocs, red Barghests and bone Serpents in the Ember Wastes.
- **Pelts to match the land.** Each March biome is home to a pelt, so a wild mount there usually wears it: polar Bears and grey Elk in the snow, storm Rocs on the Highlands, red coats in the Ember Wastes.
- **More ground to stand on.** Wild mounts can now spawn on basalt, clay and the March's own stone, cobble, moonstone and moss agate, not only grass, dirt, sand and snow.
- Nothing needs Tribal Power: without it these rules do nothing. Other mods whose biomes use the common `c:` tags still get the right mounts, as before.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21. Client and server.
